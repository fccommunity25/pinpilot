Privacy Policy

PinPilot

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Overview

PinPilot is a Pinterest content management and analytics tool. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed and handled when PinPilot connects to a Pinterest account.

Information Accessed

When authorized by the account owner, PinPilot may access information provided through the Pinterest API, including Pinterest account information, Pins, Boards, publishing information, and performance data necessary for the features being used.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Pinterest is used to manage Pinterest content, publish authorized content, organize Pins and Boards, analyze account performance, and generate reports.

Data Sharing

PinPilot does not sell personal information. Information accessed through Pinterest is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide an authorized service, comply with applicable law, or protect the security of the service.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information and authentication credentials from unauthorized access.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the intended functionality, maintain records, or meet legal obligations. Users may request deletion of information associated with PinPilot.

Pinterest

PinPilot uses Pinterest's official API and only accesses Pinterest data after authorization by the account owner. Pinterest's own privacy practices also apply to information processed by Pinterest.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PinPilot's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the PinPilot developer through the contact information provided on the PinPilot website.
